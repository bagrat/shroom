Questions and known limits

What shroom needs, and what it doesn't do yet

What it needs

  • Which Macs?macOS 14 or newer, on Apple silicon. Intel Macs, Windows and Linux can't record with shroom — but anyone can watch your videos, on any device.
  • Do I need a Cloudflare account?Yes, the person who sets shroom up does. Cloudflare asks for a card on file; a few hours of video are free. People you invite don't need one.
  • What does it cost?Only what Cloudflare charges for storage — the prices on the front page are theirs. Delete old videos and you pay less.

What it doesn't do yet

  • Editing — trimming and cutting. Pause, restart and discard are there while you record.Coming soon
  • Captions and chapters.Coming soon
  • View counts on a video's page.Coming soon
  • Deleting a video from its page — for now, from the Library on your Mac.Coming soon
  • Records a whole display, or just your camera — not one window or a part of the screen.
  • No passwords or expiry dates on links, and no links on your own domain.
  • No comments on a video's page.

Sharing

  • Who can watch my video?Anyone you give the link to. Links are long and random, and search engines are asked to leave them out — but anyone who has one can watch, and download, the video.
  • What happens if I delete a video?It's gone from its link for good: the link shows that there's no video there.
  • What if shroom disappears?Your videos and links live in your own Cloudflare account, so they keep working.

Your team

  • How do people join?You send an invite; they install shroom and accept it. Each person sees only their own videos.
  • How many?Up to 200 people in a space. One space per Mac.
