Privacy

Short, because there's little to say: we never hold your videos

Your videos

  • Where they liveIn your own Cloudflare account. They go straight from your Mac to your storage; they never pass through us, and we can't see them.
  • Who can watchAnyone with a video's link. Its page sets no cookie for viewers, counts no views, and asks search engines to leave it out.
  • Your Cloudflare tokenKept on your Mac only, in a folder only you can read. It's sent to Cloudflare and nowhere else.

The app

  • No trackingNo analytics, no crash reports, nothing sent to us.
  • UpdatesA few times a day it asks GitHub, where shroom's releases are, whether there's a newer one. GitHub sees that request the way it sees any download.
  • Your teamYour space keeps the name you give each invite, and each of your Macs' name and your user name on it, so the Members list can show them. No email addresses.

This website

  • What we countEach visit to a page: which page, what kind of browser or tool (never its full details), the site that linked here, and the country. No cookies, no addresses, nothing that ties one visit to another. Counts are kept three months.
  • Cloudflare's visit countershroom.am also runs Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits the same cookie-free way.
