Privacy

shroom is built so that we hold nothing of yours.

Updated 17 October 2026.

  • Your videos live in your own Cloudflare account. We never receive them, store them or see them. Who can watch a video is up to you: anyone with its link can.
  • The app talks to your Cloudflare account (to set it up, upload your videos and manage your space) and to GitHub (to check for updates, sending no ID or version; like any website, GitHub sees your IP address). It sends us nothing on its own: no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports. Its logs stay on your Mac unless you send us a report.
  • Reports you send. When you press Send on Report a problem or on shroom.am/feedback, what you wrote, your shroom and macOS versions, your Mac's model and the cleaned log you were shown go by email to the person who makes shroom, and nowhere else. shroom.am keeps none of it. Nothing goes unless you press Send.
  • Your Cloudflare token and card. The token you create during setup stays on your Mac, and a key made from it stays in your own Cloudflare account; neither ever reaches us. Your card goes to Cloudflare, on Cloudflare's own page, and shroom never sees it.
  • This website counts visits so we can see how people find shroom. For each page it counts the page, the kind of browser (not which one), the site that linked here (just its name), the country, and a link's ?ref= tag if it has one. It never keeps your IP address, your browser's details, the full address you came from, or a cookie. The counts are kept for three months. Cloudflare's Web Analytics also counts visits on our behalf, without cookies.
  • Your teammates. If you invite someone, their videos go to your Cloudflare account, and you can see and delete them. They can see only their own. Your space keeps its list of members in your own account: the names you give them and their Macs' names, never email addresses.
  • People watching your videos get no cookies and aren't counted or logged.
  • The demo on our home page plays from the shroom space of the person who makes shroom, the way any shroom link plays, and starts loading only once it's on your screen or close to it. Like any video page, that space gets no cookie from you and doesn't count or log you.
