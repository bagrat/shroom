shroom editor — a working prototype

Seven invented takes, spoken by a stock voice, each with a slip and “shroom cut”. The planner read the transcript and cut the slips. Play a take: it skips what's cut. Undo a cut, accept a suggestion, drag the yellow ends to trim, or drag across the sound to cut a part by hand.
