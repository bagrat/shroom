Privacy
shroom is built so that we hold nothing of yours.
  • Your videos live in your own Cloudflare account. We never receive them, store them or see them. Who can watch a video is up to you: anyone with its link can.
  • AI, if you turn it on, runs in your own Cloudflare account: it hears what’s said in your new videos to name them and add chapters and captions. What it hears stays in your space and goes when you delete the video; it never reaches us. When you say “shroom cut”, the slip is cut from what viewers get; the words cut stay in your space, and only you see them.
  • The app talks to your Cloudflare account (to set it up, upload your videos and manage your space) and to GitHub (to check for updates, sending no ID or version) …
