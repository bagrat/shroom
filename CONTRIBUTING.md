# Contributing to shroom

Thanks for looking. shroom is built by one person, Bagrat, so answers can take a few days.

**Issues** are the best way to help: something broken, something confusing, something
missing. The templates ask for your shroom and macOS versions, which saves a round trip.

**Pull requests** are welcome for bugs and small fixes. For anything larger, please open
an issue first, so we can agree on the shape before you spend your time. shroom stays
small on purpose: no SaaS, nothing the project operates, no features that need a server
of ours.

**How it's built** is in [`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), and what it does and says, state by
state, is in [`spec/MACHINE.md`](spec/MACHINE.md).

By contributing, you agree your work is released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).
