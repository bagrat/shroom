# shroom v1 — build decisions

> In-frame design calls made during the build (CLAUDE.md, Build rules). Each records what
> was chosen, why, and what was rejected, so Bagrat can review and reverse any of them at a
> checkpoint. Stack-level choices live in the stack decision (2026-07-20). Where the build
> departs from the spec, the entry goes to [the deviations](#deviations-from-the-spec)
> below, not to a B-row.

B1–B120 are indexed here one line each; their full rows (chosen, why, rejected) are in
[`history/decisions/B001-B060.md`](../history/decisions/B001-B060.md) and
[`history/decisions/B061-B120.md`](../history/decisions/B061-B120.md). From B121 on, the rows
are here in full, and a new decision is a new row at the end of that table.
