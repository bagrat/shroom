- Edit your videos in shroom: right-click a video in the Library and choose Edit… to trim its ends, cut out a stretch, and undo. The link stays the same, and viewers see the edit once it's saved.
- The Edit in shroom button on your video's page opens it straight in the editor.
- Drop a marker while recording with ⌥⌘M, or from the menu while paused, and find it in the editor.
- Turn on AI and your space names your videos, adds chapters to the seek bar, and writes captions viewers can switch on, all on your own Cloudflare account.
- Slipped mid-sentence? Say “shroom cut”, say the line again, and the slip is cut from your video. You can put any cut back from the video's page.
- The Library suggests videos worth cleaning up, from how much they're watched and how much room they take, and deleting one is a click with Undo.
- The shroom command lets you, or your coding agent, record, invite people and tidy videos from Terminal. Recording from it stays off until you switch it on in Settings.
- While your video finishes uploading, its page shows that the link already works, so you can share it right away.
