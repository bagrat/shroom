- On your video's page, you can now edit its chapters: click the chapters icon beside the title to rename one, move where it starts, remove it or add one at the playhead, and choose whether they show in the seek bar. Every change saves as you make it. Once you've edited them, they're yours: shroom never renames over them, and they move with your trims and cuts.
- shroom's own windows (the Library, the editor, Settings, Send feedback) now show in your recordings like any other app's, so a recording of something going wrong in shroom shows what happened. The countdown, the marker toast and the popover still stay out of the video.
- An invite to a space now says which space it is, under who invited you.
- A button that moves just as you click it, like Update arriving in the popover, no longer takes that click. The next click works as usual.
- After you cut a video, its captions and chapters on its page now follow the cut.
- Moving along the editor's ruler no longer leaves traces of its time label behind.
- Hardening throughout: the app, its updates, the installer, the shroom command, your video's page and shroom.am all got a careful going-over.
